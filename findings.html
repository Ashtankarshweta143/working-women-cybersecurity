Findings & Recommendations

Exploring the results and possible improvements in cyber safety awareness.

Research Findings

The survey data is currently being collected. Once the responses are received and analysed, the major findings of the study will be presented here.

Research in Progress

The findings, charts and statistical results will be updated after the completion of data collection and analysis.

Key Areas of Analysis

  • Digital platform usage among working women.
  • Awareness of common cyber threats.
  • Experiences with online fraud and cyber harassment.
  • Knowledge of privacy and data protection.
  • Awareness of cybercrime reporting methods.

Recommendations

Based on the objectives of this study, the following areas can be considered for improving cyber safety:

  • Promote cybersecurity awareness programmes.
  • Encourage the use of strong passwords and two-factor authentication.
  • Educate users about phishing and online fraud.
  • Encourage safe handling of personal and professional information.
  • Improve awareness of cybercrime reporting procedures.
