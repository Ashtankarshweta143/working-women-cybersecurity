INTRODUCTION

Why Cyber Safety Matters

Digital technology has become an essential part of professional and personal life. Working women regularly use emails, social media, online banking, communication platforms and other digital services.

However, increased digital participation also brings cybersecurity risks such as phishing, identity theft, online harassment, privacy violations and financial fraud.

This research focuses on understanding these challenges and identifying the importance of cybersecurity awareness and workplace support for women.
