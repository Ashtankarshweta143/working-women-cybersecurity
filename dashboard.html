Key Findings / Analysis

  1. 14 responses analysed
    Only responses numbered 31 to 44 are included in this dashboard.
  2. 57.1% use digital platforms several times a day
    8 out of 14 respondents selected 'Several times a day'.
  3. 42.9% are somewhat aware of cybersecurity
    6 respondents chose 'Somewhat Aware'; 28.6% said 'Very Aware'.
  4. 64.3% say cybersecurity awareness is extremely important
    9 respondents selected 'Extremely Important'; all 14 rated it at least moderately important.
  5. 28.6% reported facing a cyber threat
    4 respondents answered 'Yes'; 1 respondent was unsure.
  6. Phishing is the most selected threat
    5 respondents selected phishing emails / fake links; 4 selected financial or online transaction fraud.
  7. 42.9% are not aware of their organisation's policy
    6 respondents selected 'Not Aware'; 5 said a policy is clearly defined.
  8. 57.1% need more legal guidance or are not aware
    5 need guidance and 3 are not aware of legal provisions.
  9. 14.3% reported an incident to the cybercrime portal/police
    2 respondents selected police/cybercrime reporting; 5 did not report and 7 marked not applicable.
  10. 35.7% reported mental stress as an impact
    5 respondents selected mental stress/anxiety; multiple selections were allowed.
