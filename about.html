About Our Project

Understanding the importance of cyber safety among working women.

Introduction

Digital technology has become an important part of everyday professional and personal life. Working women use digital platforms for communication, banking, online services and workplace activities.

However, increased digital usage also brings risks such as online fraud, identity theft, privacy violations, phishing and cyber harassment.

This project aims to understand the level of cyber safety awareness among working women and explore their experiences with digital threats.

Objectives of the Study

  • To understand digital technology usage among working women.
  • To examine their awareness of common cyber threats.
  • To identify experiences related to online fraud and cyber harassment.
  • To understand awareness of privacy and data protection.
  • To explore the need for cybersecurity education and organisational support.

Purpose of the Study

The main purpose of this study is to highlight the importance of cybersecurity awareness and encourage safer digital practices among working women.
